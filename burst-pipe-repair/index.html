Few water damage events are as common, or as potentially devastating to a property, as a pipe that bursts. At 4 to 8 gallons a minute, just one burst can send 240 to 480 gallons each hour into your ceilings, walls and flooring. Pipes burst from freezing, old plumbing, too much water pressure, corrosion or a physical hit, and in every case a fast emergency response is what keeps water from ruining flooring, structural materials, building systems and your belongings.

When a pipe bursts in Sheboygan or the surrounding area, Sheboygan Restoration runs an emergency response around the clock. Our restoration technicians can help you locate and shut off your water supply, and we coordinate with licensed plumbers so the plumbing repair is done properly. Once the water source is stopped, we immediately begin extraction with truck-mounted and portable equipment, removing standing water from all affected areas as quickly as possible.

After the water is out, we dry every material that took on moisture: finished floors, subflooring, wood framing, insulation and drywall. Gravity carries the water through walls, floors and ceilings, so damage from one burst commonly shows up in several rooms and on different floors. Hidden moisture leads to mold and a deteriorating structure, so we use moisture detection equipment and thermal imaging to map the water's path and make sure all affected areas get dried.

Signs You Need Burst Pipe Repair in Sheboygan

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Stale or earthy smells coming from a cabinet or wall cavity
  • Tap water that turns brown, yellow or cloudy
  • Faucets that give only a trickle, or nothing, during a freeze
  • Wet spots, bulging or staining on walls or ceilings
  • Water pressure that falls off abruptly at every fixture
  • Hissing or rushing noises in the walls with every tap turned off
  • An unusually high water bill that points to a hidden leak

Maintenance Tips for Burst Pipe Repair

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Every few years, ask a licensed plumber to look over the system for corrosion and worn parts
  • If your home still has polybutylene or aging galvanized steel lines, replace them before one gives out
  • When it freezes, let taps drip and open the cabinets under sinks to let warm air reach the pipes
  • Put leak sensors by the water heater and along the main supply line
  • Monitor your water pressure, because excessive pressure (above 80 PSI) strains pipes and fittings
  • Wrap any bare pipe that runs through a crawl space, garage or attic without heat
