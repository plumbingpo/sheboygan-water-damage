A sump pump that quits, a sewer that backs up, a pipe that lets go, rising river water, storm surge or a hard rain can each flood a property, and the damage should be handled by professionals without delay. Expect gray water (Category 2) or black water (Category 3) in almost any flood, and with it bacteria, contamination and potentially hazardous substances that threaten your health. For flood emergencies anywhere in Sheboygan, Sheboygan Restoration is available 24/7 to extract the water, disinfect affected areas and bring the property back safely.

We start with fast water extraction, using truck-mounted and submersible pumps that can move thousands of gallons per hour. Next our technicians, who hold IICRC certification, judge the level of contamination and choose a sanitizing plan that covers HEPA air filtration, antimicrobial application and disinfecting each surface that got wet. We tear out and properly dispose of what cannot be saved, such as soaked carpet padding, contaminated drywall and ruined insulation.

Once the space is extracted and sanitized, our team brings in commercial drying gear, namely low-grain refrigerant (LGR) dehumidifiers plus high-velocity air movers, so the structural components dry all the way through. We check moisture levels every day with calibrated meters and thermal imaging so the drying is complete and mold does not get a start. From start to finish we document each step for your insurance claim, with the moisture maps, equipment logs, photos and scope-of-work reports adjusters ask for.

Warning Signs for Flood Cleanup

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • A layer of silt, dirt or debris on the floor after water recedes
  • Cracked foundation walls, low doorways or window wells letting water inside
  • Ground so saturated that puddles sit against the foundation walls
  • Water pooled on any floor of the building, no matter where it came from
  • Basement flooding after the sump pump quit in a downpour
  • Roof or wall openings left by a storm, with rain pouring through
  • Soft, wet drywall or carpeting that is soaked through

Maintenance Tips for Flood Cleanup

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Being outside a designated flood zone does not rule out flooding, so think about flood coverage
  • Wherever flooding is likely, get appliances and electrical equipment up above floor level
  • Check that grading on all sides of the house carries water away from the foundation
  • Coat basement walls with waterproofing and seal any cracks in the foundation
  • Have a backwater valve fitted to the sewer line so sewage cannot flow back inside
  • Add a sump pump with battery backup, since power outages and storms tend to arrive together

Why Hire a Professional for Flood Cleanup?

Getting the standing water out is only one part of cleaning up after a flood. Floodwater carries bacteria, sewage, chemicals and other contaminants that are serious health hazards. Protecting your family from E. coli, hepatitis and similar waterborne illness takes proper containment, correct use of protective equipment (PPE) and proven sanitizing methods, all things professional teams train for.

Beyond the health concerns, a poor flood cleanup often leaves hidden moisture that turns into severe mold growth within days. Professional technicians use moisture meters, thermal imaging and psychrometric calculations to make sure every affected area, including wall cavities, subfloors and framing, reaches an acceptable drying standard. Drying by measurement is the one dependable method for avoiding secondary damage, which can add thousands to the repair bill.
