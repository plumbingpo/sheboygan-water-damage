Maintenance Tips for Storm Damage Repair

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Make sure the ground around the house slopes away and downspouts discharge well clear of it
  • Know how to shut off your main water supply in case a storm damages a pipe
  • Cut back limbs that hang over or close to the house so wind cannot throw them into it
  • Make sure the attic is properly ventilated and insulated to prevent ice dams
  • Have your roof checked annually and fix vulnerable areas ahead of the stormy months
  • Keep gutters and downspouts clear so water drains properly in heavy rain

Signs You Need Storm Damage Repair in Sheboygan

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Rooms that start to smell stale or moldy following a storm
  • Water getting past doors, windows and wall penetrations when wind pushes the rain
  • Standing water in attic spaces, especially by skylights and chimneys
  • Bubbling paint and soft, drooping spots overhead once the rain stops
  • Flashing that is bent, loose or missing at roof edges, valleys and vents
  • Shingles that are gone, cracked or curled up when you look from the yard
  • Sagging or detached gutters that spill rainwater down the siding

What a Professional Brings to Storm Damage Repair

Storm damage creates complex, connected repair needs that take experienced restoration professionals to handle properly. A roof leak that looks minor from inside may have done extensive hidden damage to insulation, roof decking and wall cavities, and that leads to mold growth and structural deterioration if it is not found and dried. With moisture meters and thermal cameras, trained technicians follow the route water took and find all the areas it reached.

Emergency board-up and tarping also take proper technique to be effective. An improperly installed tarp can create new problems by channeling water or failing under wind load. Experienced in storm mitigation, our team picks appropriate methods and materials to keep the building protected until permanent repair work can be scheduled.
