Signs It Is Time for Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • An outlet, a light fixture or the ceiling above has water dripping out of it.
  • Water has reached the electrical panel, appliances or power strips on the floor.
  • Water is still flowing and you cannot find or reach the shutoff valve.
  • Water from a floor above is streaming down interior walls.
  • Carpet, drywall or hardwood has been soaked for more than a few hours.
  • The tank on your water heater gave out and its contents are spreading through the room.
  • You came home to water and do not know how long it ran.

Emergency Water Damage Restoration Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Going away longer than a few days? Close the main water valve first.
  • Replace old supply hoses on washers, toilets and faucets before they burst.
  • Wrap plumbing in cold, unheated parts of the house so a hard freeze cannot crack it.
  • Keep the number of an emergency restoration company saved in your phone before you need it.
  • Locate the main shutoff valve now, and show each person at home how to turn it off.
  • Sinks, dishwashers, washing machines and water heaters are all good spots for a leak sensor.
