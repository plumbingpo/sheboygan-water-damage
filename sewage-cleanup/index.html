Few emergencies in a building are as hazardous as sewage backing up into it. Anyone who comes in contact with contaminated material is exposed to parasites, bacteria and viruses, among them giardia, E. coli, salmonella and hepatitis, all carried in raw sewage and all dangerous. Sheboygan Restoration provides emergency sewage cleanup throughout Sheboygan, responding 24/7 with properly trained technicians, appropriate personal protective equipment and EPA-registered disinfectants to decontaminate your property safely.

Water damage comes in categories, and sewage sits in the worst one: Category 3, called black water, the highest level of contamination and danger. Because of it, cleanup follows the toughest rules in the trade, which means full protective gear, barriers to contain the area, tearing out any porous material that met sewage and disinfecting all that is left. We work to the IICRC S500 standard that governs Category 3 losses, with the goal of returning your property to a condition safe to occupy.

The cleanup process involves removing all standing sewage, extracting contaminated water from structural materials, removing and properly disposing of every porous material that contacted sewage (carpet, padding, drywall, insulation), thoroughly disinfecting hard surfaces, applying antimicrobial treatments and completing structural drying to prevent secondary mold growth. Lingering sewage odor is eliminated with thermal fogging and industrial hydroxyl generators, which we run in the affected area.

How to Prevent Sewage Cleanup Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Stormwater from a sump or downspout should never drain into the sewer, since the extra volume invites a backup
  • Toilet paper is the only thing to flush, and that rules out wipes sold as flushable, which still clog
  • Roots from trees close to the sewer line work their way into pipes and block them, so have them cleared
  • Never pour grease, oil or food waste down your drains, since these cause blockages
  • In an emergency a plumber needs the cleanout, so learn where yours is and keep it in good shape
  • Fit a strainer over each sink and shower drain so hair and debris stay out of the pipes

Why Sewage Cleanup Is Not a DIY Job

This is one cleanup you should never take on yourself. The biological hazards in raw sewage require specialized training, proper personal protective equipment (Tyvek suits, respirators, protective gloves and boots) and EPA-registered disinfection products that are not available to consumers. Hepatitis, gastrointestinal disease and infections of the skin or respiratory system are among the serious illnesses these pathogens cause.

Contaminated materials count as biohazard waste, and disposing of them properly is part of a professional cleanup. Putting carpet, insulation or drywall soaked with sewage out with household trash breaks the law and endangers sanitation workers and your community. Certified restoration companies maintain proper waste disposal protocols and relationships with licensed biohazard waste disposal facilities.

Nobody, pets included, should walk through the area the sewage reached. Do not flush, run faucets or start the washer or dishwasher, because added water feeds the backup. Switching off the heating and cooling and the affected circuits is wise, provided you stay clear of the contaminated water while doing it. Temporary relocation is what we usually recommend, particularly for a backup that contaminated a large part of the home. Both the decontamination chemicals and pathogens in the air can be a risk to your health. Our recommendation will depend on what we find in your particular home.
Most backups start with a blockage in the home's sewer line. Roots from nearby trees, flushed wipes and accumulated grease account for a large share of those clogs. Sometimes an aging pipe cracks or caves in, or a storm overwhelms the city sewer and forces sewage up the floor drains. To know for certain, have the line camera-inspected once cleanup is finished.

Signs You Need Sewage Cleanup in Sheboygan

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Every sink, tub and shower draining slowly even after you plunge them
  • Drain openings or basement slabs coated with visible sewage residue
  • A cleanout that weeps sewage, or wet foul patches in the lawn along the sewer path
  • Toilet water with waste in it soaking into carpet or running across the floor
  • Lower levels or drain openings giving off a heavy sewage odor, sometimes like rotten eggs
  • Multiple drains in your home backing up at the same time
  • Gurgling sounds from toilets or drains when other fixtures are used
